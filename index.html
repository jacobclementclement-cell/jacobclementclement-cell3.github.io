Akira (1988)

Akira is a landmark 1988 Japanese animated cyberpunk film directed by Katsuhiro Otomo, based on his manga of the same name. Set in a dystopian Neo-Tokyo in 2019, the story follows Kaneda, the leader of a biker gang, whose childhood friend Tetsuo gains destructive psychic powers after a motorcycle accident. As Tetsuo's abilities spiral out of control, Kaneda and a group of rebels must confront him while uncovering government conspiracies. The film is celebrated for its groundbreaking animation, complex themes, and massive cultural influence.

Art Director: Toshiharu Mizutani
Chief Animator: Takashi Nakamura
Scenario: Izo Hashimoto
Music: Shoji Yamashiro
Producer: Ryohei Suzuki
